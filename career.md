## Professional Journey

Arent van 't Spijker has established himself as a leading voice in information management and digital transformation over the past two decades. His career trajectory showcases a unique blend of practical experience, academic knowledge, and innovative thinking that has shaped the field of enterprise information management.

### Current Positions

#### Partner at Highberg
As a Partner at Highberg, Arent leads digital transformation initiatives for major enterprises. He helps organizations navigate the complex landscape of data management, digital innovation, and business transformation. His role involves strategic planning, implementation oversight, and ensuring successful delivery of complex digital projects.

#### Senior Trainer at Gladwell Academy
In his role as a Senior Trainer, Arent shares his extensive knowledge with professionals seeking to enhance their understanding of information management and digital transformation. His courses combine theoretical foundations with practical applications, drawing from his vast experience in the field.
