# Arent van 't Spijker

## Information Management Expert & Author

![Arent van 't Spijker](https://api.placeholder.com/400/300)

Arent van 't Spijker is a distinguished information management expert, author, and innovation specialist with over 20 years of experience in the field. Currently serving as a Partner at Highberg, he has established himself as a thought leader in digital transformation and data-driven business models.

### Notable Works

- "Continuous Innovation: How successful organizations continuously develop, scale, and embed innovations to lead tomorrow's markets" (2021)
- "The New Oil: Using Innovative Business Models to turn Data Into Profit" (2014)
- "Enterprise Information Management in Practice: Managing Data and Leveraging Profits in Today's Complex Business Environment"

### Current Role

As a Partner at Highberg, Arent helps organizations navigate the complex landscape of digital transformation and data management. He is also a Senior Trainer at Gladwell Academy, where he shares his expertise with the next generation of information management professionals.
